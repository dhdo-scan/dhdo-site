Why Lake Charles

Why Lake Charles Homes Need Documentation

Lake Charles doesn't need a hypothetical to explain why documentation matters — this city has lived it. Homeowners here don't ask "what if" about hurricanes; they ask "when."

01

A Local Reality, Not a Hypothetical

In August 2020, Hurricane Laura made landfall near Cameron as a Category 4 storm, tearing through Calcasieu Parish with catastrophic wind damage. Six weeks later, Hurricane Delta followed nearly the same path. Fifteen years earlier, Hurricane Rita struck the same region in 2005.

02

Records Get Lost in the Chaos

After a storm, roofs get replaced, contents get discarded before anyone thought to photograph them, and receipts get lost in the chaos of evacuation. A homeowner reconstructing what they owned from memory, weeks or months later, is starting from a disadvantage that was avoidable.

03

Built Before You Need It

A DHDO scan exists so that disadvantage never happens. It's built before the storm, stored off-site, and ready the moment a roof leaks, a fence goes down, or a home floods — regardless of what happens to the physical house itself.

Our Process

How Your Lake Charles Scan Works

Booking takes one phone call. From there, we handle everything — most Lake Charles homes and businesses are fully documented in a single visit.

1
Step One

Schedule Your Scan

Call or fill out the form. Every inquiry gets a response within 12 hours, and most scans are booked within days.

2
Step Two

We Scan Your Property

One visit, start to finish. A DHDO operator walks every room, closet, garage, and outbuilding — or every classroom and common area, for a business — with our 3D camera. Nothing is touched or moved.

3
Step Three

Assets Get Tagged

Furniture, electronics, appliances, and valuables are tagged directly in the model with make, model, serial number, and purchase date — plus a link to the item online where one exists, so an adjuster or contractor can see exactly what it is and what it currently sells for. If it’s listed online, we’ll find it. DHDO documents; it doesn’t appraise or assign values.

4
Step Four

Your Record Is Delivered

Your finished walkthrough, floor plans, still photos, and tagged inventory land in your private, secure client portal — ready to share with an adjuster, contractor, or family member the moment you need it.
