What We Do

The Call You Never Have to Make

The hardest call in your book is the one where a client's claim is disputed and there's nothing to point to. DHDO closes that gap before it opens. Our team scans the property, tags the assets, and delivers a timestamped digital record your client — and you — can reference the day it matters.

In Lake Charles, this isn't hypothetical. After Hurricanes Laura and Delta, thousands of homeowners had to prove what they'd lost from memory and a few phone photos — and many settled for less because of it. A pre-loss record is the difference between remembering a room and documenting it.

DHDO is a documentation tool, not an insurance producer, adjuster, or inspector. We don't determine coverage, assign value, or make underwriting calls — we document what's there, so the people who do have a solid, dated record to start from.

Pre-Loss, Not Post-Claim

Every scan happens before a loss, not after — the difference between a record insurers can rely on and a homeowner's memory of a room that no longer exists.

Done For You, Not DIY

Home inventory apps ask homeowners to do the work, and most never finish it. DHDO's team does the scan, the tagging, and the delivery — nothing for your client to maintain.

A Record Every Party Can Reference

One secure link. Your client, your office, and the adjuster can each view the same documented record — no chasing photos or receipts after the fact.

Measured, Not Estimated

Hand-measured inspections leave room for error — overshoot the footprint and a client pays for coverage they don't need; undershoot it and they're underinsured on what they do have. A DHDO scan captures exact measurements from day one, so the file starts from a precise record, not a guess.
