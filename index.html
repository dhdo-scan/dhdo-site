What We Do

What's Included in a DHDO Home Scan

DHDO uses Matterport 3D scanning technology to create an interactive, navigable record of your entire home — every room, every corner, every item.

Each scan is enriched with tagged assets, serial numbers, purchase dates, and receipts where provided. The result is a living document your insurance adjuster can navigate, your attorney can reference, and your family can rely on.

Full-Home 3D Scanning

Immersive scans of every room, closet, garage, and outbuilding — capturing your home in stunning, navigable detail.

Tagged Asset Documentation

Every item catalogued with make, model, serial number, and purchase date — embedded directly in the scan.

Secure Delivery & Access

Your completed documentation delivered via a secure, encrypted link — available anywhere, anytime, on any device. Your data is never sold, and is shared only as needed to deliver your service.

Your Secure Client Portal

Every scan, detailed asset images, your tagged inventory, signed documents, and invoices in one private login — ready the instant you need them. Open your portal →

Our Process

Six Steps.
Two to Three Weeks.
A Lifetime of Protection.

1
Today

Welcome & Sign-Up

You complete your client information and onboarding packet. We confirm your scan date within one business day — the clock starts from there.

2
3–4 Business Days

Preparation Window

Gather receipts for major purchases — furniture, electronics, appliances, jewelry, tools. List every item you want tagged and tidy each room for scan day.

3
Scan Day

We Come to You

Our team arrives on your scheduled date. The scan takes one to three hours depending on home size — you don't need to follow us room to room.

4
5–7 Business Days

Processing & Labeling

We process the 3D scan and attach asset tags using your receipts and item information — make, model, serial number, and purchase date embedded in the scan.

5
Day 7 Post-Scan

Receipt Cutoff

Receipts or item info received after the 7th business day following your scan cannot be added without an additional labeling fee. Get everything in before the deadline.

6
Delivery

Your Documentation, Delivered

You receive your secure 3D scan link and complete asset documentation. Payment is due upon delivery — all major cards, ACH, and check accepted.
