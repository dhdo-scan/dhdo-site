What We Do

What's Included in a DHDO Home Scan

DHDO uses Matterport 3D scanning technology to create an interactive, navigable record of your entire home — every room, every corner, every item.

Each scan is enriched with tagged assets, serial numbers, purchase dates, and replacement values. The result is a living document your insurance adjuster can navigate, your attorney can reference, and your family can rely on.

Tagged Asset Documentation

Secure Delivery & Access

Your completed documentation delivered via a secure, shareable link — available anywhere, anytime, on any device.

Why It Matters

Prepared Before You Ever Need It

Most homeowners only realize they needed documentation after the worst has already happened. We exist to make sure that moment never catches you unprepared.

01

Insurance Claims

Insurers require proof of ownership and value. A complete DHDO file turns a disputed claim into a settled one — receipts, photos, and a 3D walkthrough backing every item.

02

Hurricane Season & Storm Claims

In Louisiana, the question isn't if — it's when. Wind, surge, and flood claims come down to proof of what you had before the storm. A DHDO record gives your adjuster a complete, timestamped walkthrough of every room and every item — backed up off-site and accessible the moment you need it.

03

Estate & Legal

A complete, timestamped inventory of your home's contents is invaluable for estate planning, divorce proceedings, or any situation where an accurate asset record is required.

Our Process

Six Steps.
Two to Three Weeks.
A Lifetime of Protection.

1
Today

Welcome & Sign-Up

You complete your client information form and submit your onboarding packet. We confirm your scan date within one business day — the clock starts from there.

2
3–4 Business Days

Preparation Window

Gather receipts for major purchases — furniture, electronics, appliances, jewelry, tools. Make a list of every item you want tagged and tidy each room so we can move through cleanly on scan day.

3
Scan Day

We Come to You

Our team arrives on your scheduled date. The scan takes one to three hours depending on home size. You don't need to follow us room to room — relax, work from home, do whatever is easiest. We handle the rest.

4
5–7 Business Days

Processing & Labeling

We process the 3D scan and attach asset tags using your receipts and item information. Every tagged item gets its make, model, serial number, purchase date, and replacement value embedded directly into the scan.

5
Day 7 Post-Scan

Receipt Cutoff

Any receipts or item information received after the 7th business day following your scan cannot be added without an additional labeling fee. Get everything to us before the deadline for the cleanest result.

6
Delivery

Your Documentation, Delivered

You receive your secure 3D scan link and complete asset documentation. Payment is due upon delivery. We accept all major credit cards, ACH, and check.
